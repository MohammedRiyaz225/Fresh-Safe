Our Story & Mission

From Our Farms to
Your Family

We're on a mission to revolutionize India's food supply chain by connecting local farmers directly to your doorstep. Every bite you take carries the love, care, and dedication of thousands of farming families we proudly partner with.

Our Mission

To empower Indian farmers with fair prices and sustainable livelihoods while delivering the freshest, safest, and most nutritious farm produce directly to every household, building trust through complete transparency from soil to shelf.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted farm-to-home platform by 2030 — creating a thriving ecosystem where farmers prosper, consumers eat healthy, and our food systems are regenerative, transparent, and accessible to every family across the nation.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These four pillars guide every decision we make and every product we deliver.

Freshness

Harvested at dawn, delivered by noon. Our cold-chain logistics ensure every item reaches you at peak freshness, just hours from the farm.

Transparency

Know your farmer. Scan QR codes on every package to trace origin, see harvest dates, and meet the family who grew your food.

Sustainability

100% compostable packaging, zero-waste warehouses, and organic farming partnerships — because healthy food needs a healthy planet.

Community

Fair prices for farmers, free nutrition drives for schools, and women empowerment programs — we grow together, not alone.
